Numbering System Key (Edher 2026)

Format: TIMG.{Quadrant}.I{n} (e.g., TIMG.NW.I1 = insula at the NW corner of the CM/DM intersection). For individual houses within an insula, append .H{n} (e.g., TIMG.SE.I20.H1).

Quadrant origin: The Cardo Maximus (between C5-C6) and Decumanus Maximus (between R6-R7) divide the Trajanic city into four quadrants. Insula I1 in each quadrant is the block touching both axes.

Numbering direction: Insulae are numbered outward from the CM/DM intersection, proceeding row by row toward the city walls.

Quadrant sizes: NW = 5 cols x 6 rows = 30 insulae. NE = 6 cols x 6 rows = 36 insulae. SW = 5 cols x 6 rows = 30 insulae. SE = 6 cols x 6 rows = 36 insulae. Total = 132.

Building prefixes: H = House (domestic), P = Public building (forum, theatre, library), B = Baths (thermal complexes).

Extramural quadrants: E-NW, E-NE, E-SW, E-SE mirror the intramural divisions for houses outside the Trajanic walls. Houses that cannot yet be placed on the grid are listed under Unlocated.

Blue cells mark insulae with at least one house in the project database. A thick border and ✓ mark houses whose full record is published in the catalog. A small drawn tag marks houses whose plan has been redrawn as measurable vector geometry (Analysis: drawn in the catalog). Gray cells are blocks with no individually documented house in the database; many hold buildings that are simply not yet catalogued house by house.

Monuments (the Forum, Theatre, baths, library, and markets) are placed by overlaying the quadrant grid on the georeferenced city plan, so their blocks match what a visitor sees on the ground. Where a monument's footprint covers a block that also carries a house record (around the Forum and Theatre), the block is shaded as the monument and the house record remains available in the block's detail panel while its placement is checked against the original reports.

Purple G:## labels = Germain 1969 insula number. Boustrophedon confirmed for R1-R6, computed for R7-R12.

Sources: Wilson 2001 annotated plan (C1-C11 x R1-R12 grid); Germain 1969 numbered plan and mosaic catalog; Ballu 1903 street-by-street descriptions; Ballu 1911 quarter descriptions; Rebuffat 1969/1974 peristyle house catalog. House assignments and records: the project house database (Edher 2026). Monument positions cross-checked against the project's georeferenced plan overlays (satellite imagery with the published city plans).
