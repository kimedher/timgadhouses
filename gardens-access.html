    Sources. The drawing (Sep 25, 2026), traced from Ballu 1897 I: Pl. XXXVII and fig. 36 (printed p. 224) and BCB 1905: fig. 40 (pp. 88-92). Room names follow the drawing; Ballu calls the court an atrium and the three-bay room the tablinum.

    Method. Each room, the court and each portico is one space, the street is space zero, and every doorway or open column line is one link. Depth, connections, control (CV), integration (RRA) and rings are recalculated live for each reading. Interaction labels (after Fisher) and visibility scores come from the drawing's analysis with all doors drawn.
