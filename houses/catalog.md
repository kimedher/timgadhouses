The catalog below lists every house in the project database, {{ site.data.houses.count }} at the last count, from the peristyle houses that made it into the guidebooks to buildings known only from a line in an excavation report. Each entry carries the identifier it holds in the [interactive grid](/grid/), so a record here can be traced to its block in the city.

Records vary in depth, and that is deliberate. Some of these houses have been studied for more than a century; others were uncovered, noted, and never drawn. Publishing the thin records beside the rich ones shows where the corpus actually stands, and where the work still is. Each record also carries a confidence rating, my own assessment of how securely the entry can be tied to a known, locatable structure.

Filter by location within the city, by building type, or by confidence. The search box covers names, alternate names, and identifiers.
