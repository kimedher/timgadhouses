Timgad City Map

The city's blocks, streets, gates and public buildings, with the houses drawn so far in blue over the satellite imagery. Zoom in on a house to see its courts, mosaics and paving, stairs, basins and wells, and economic or commercial spaces. Switch the base layer to "Plain plan" to see the map without the imagery, and see the insula grid for the block-by-block reference system.
