Timgad City Map

The city's blocks, streets, gates and public buildings, with the houses drawn so far in blue over the satellite imagery. Zoom in on a house to see its walls and doorways, graded by how clearly they show on the ground: solid where certain, dashed where probable, dotted where uncertain, with its courts, mosaics, stairs and water features. Switch the base layer to "Plain plan" to see the drawings without the imagery, and see the insula grid for the block-by-block reference system.
