Timgad — Live GIS Map

A live, growing map: houses appear here one by one as their walls are traced from satellite imagery. Zoom in on a traced house for its courts, mosaics & water features. Switch the base layer to "Plain plan" to see the drawings without the imagery, and see the insula grid for the block-by-block reference system.
