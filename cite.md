## Citing the site

If you use text, images, or data from this project in your own work, please cite it. A suggested format:

> Edher, Kim. *Houses of Roman Timgad*. 2026–. https://timgadhouses.org

For a specific page, add the page title and date accessed:

> Edher, Kim. "Why Timgad." *Houses of Roman Timgad*, 2026. Accessed [date]. https://timgadhouses.org/why-timgad/

Individual blog posts and datasets will carry their own citation blocks with complete metadata when they are added.

## Licensing

Written content and images produced for this site are released under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), which means you can share, adapt, and republish the material, including for commercial purposes, as long as you give appropriate credit.

Data released through the site, including the forthcoming house catalog, will be released under the same license unless noted otherwise.

The underlying code for this site is released under the [MIT License](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT), and the full source lives on [GitHub](https://github.com/{{ site.github_username }}/timgadhouses).

Third-party images, including historical photographs and images drawn from Wikimedia Commons, are used under their own licenses and credited on the pages where they appear.

## Corrections and contributions

This is a work in progress, and errors are inevitable. If you spot a typo, a misattribution, or a substantive problem with an argument, please let us know. The two easiest paths are:

- **Email.** Write to [km2133@student.ubc.ca](mailto:km2133@student.ubc.ca) with the page URL and what needs fixing.
- **GitHub.** Open an issue in the [project repository](https://github.com/{{ site.github_username }}/timgadhouses/issues) if you prefer public, tracked corrections.

Substantive contributions, including additional scholarship for the bibliography, photographs you have permission to share, or corrections to house identifiers, are very welcome. A formal contribution protocol will be published on the Methods page once the site grows beyond its initial phase.

## A note on the work in progress

Pages on this site carry a *Last updated* timestamp near the top. Where claims or data may shift as the dissertation research continues, that is noted directly on the page. Scholarly accuracy matters more than appearing finished, and this site is designed to let readers see the work as it develops, not only after it is done.
